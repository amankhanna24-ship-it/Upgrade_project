# NovaShop Returns Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026. Applies to orders delivered in the United Kingdom.

## RET-1 Standard return window
You can return most items within 30 calendar days of the delivery date for a full refund, provided the item is unopened, unused and in its original sealed packaging. The 30 days are counted from the date the carrier marks the parcel as delivered, not the date the order was placed. Once the 30 days have passed, NovaShop no longer accepts the item as a change-of-mind return, regardless of its condition.

## RET-2 Opened and used items
Opened items can still be returned within the 30-day window, with category-specific conditions:
- Laptops, smartphones and monitors that have been opened or powered on must be returned within 14 days of delivery, with all accessories and the original box. A 10% restocking fee is deducted unless the item is faulty.
- Over-ear headphones, keyboards, mice, smartwatches and power banks that have been opened may be returned within 30 days with no restocking fee, as long as they are complete and undamaged.
- In-ear audio products (earbuds) cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless they are faulty.

## RET-3 Items damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the item and the outer packaging. We will send a prepaid return label and offer either a full refund or a replacement, at your choice, with no restocking fee. Damage reported more than 7 days after delivery is not treated as damaged-on-arrival. It is assessed as a warranty claim under the manufacturer warranty or an active NovaCare plan instead, which means repair or replacement rather than a refund.

## RET-4 Faulty items
If an item develops a fault within 30 days of delivery, you can choose a refund, repair or replacement. After 30 days, faults are handled under the manufacturer warranty (see the Warranty Policy) or under NovaCare if you bought it. Damage you caused, for example by dropping the item or spilling liquid on it, is not a fault.

## RET-5 NovaShop Plus members
Customers on the NovaShop Plus loyalty tier get an extended 45-day return window for unopened, sealed items. The extension does not apply to opened items, which keep the windows in RET-2, and it does not apply to damaged or faulty items, which follow RET-3 and RET-4.

## RET-6 Items that cannot be returned
The following cannot be returned for a change of mind: digital gift cards, software licence keys once revealed, earbuds with a broken hygiene seal, and any item that has been physically damaged or modified by the customer.

## RET-7 How to start a return
Start a return from Orders in your NovaShop account, or ask support with your order number. You will receive a return reference and, where the return is free, a prepaid label. Items must be dropped off within 7 days of the return being approved, otherwise the return reference expires.
