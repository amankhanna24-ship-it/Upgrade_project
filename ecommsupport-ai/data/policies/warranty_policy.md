# Manufacturer Warranty Policy

## WAR-1 Warranty length by category
Every product sold by NovaShop carries a manufacturer warranty starting on the delivery date:
- Laptops, smartphones, headphones, earbuds and smartwatches: 12 months
- Keyboards and mice: 24 months
- Power banks: 18 months
- Monitors: 36 months
The exact length for each product is also shown on its product page.

## WAR-2 What the manufacturer warranty covers
The manufacturer warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, such as a laptop that stops charging, a screen with dead pixels above the manufacturer threshold, or a keyboard with unresponsive keys. It does not cover accidental damage (drops, cracked screens, liquid spills), normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage, loss or theft, or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.

## WAR-3 How warranty claims are resolved
Warranty claims are resolved by repair first. If the item cannot be repaired, it is replaced with the same or an equivalent model. A refund under warranty is only offered if neither repair nor replacement is possible within 30 days of the claim being accepted. Warranty claims do not reopen the return window.

## WAR-4 Proof of purchase
Your NovaShop order number is your proof of purchase. Warranty claims for items bought elsewhere cannot be handled by NovaShop.

## WAR-5 Batteries
Battery capacity that falls below 80% of its original rating within the first 12 months counts as a defect. Gradual battery wear after 12 months is treated as normal wear and tear.
