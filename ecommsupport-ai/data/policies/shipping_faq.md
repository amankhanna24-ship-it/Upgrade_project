# Shipping FAQ

## SHP-1 Delivery options and costs
Standard delivery (3 to 5 business days) is free on orders over £40 and costs £3.99 otherwise. Next-day delivery costs £6.99 for orders placed before 2pm, Monday to Friday. NovaShop Plus members get free next-day delivery on all orders.

## SHP-2 Dispatch times
In-stock items ordered before 2pm on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders with a status of processing have not left our warehouse yet. Out-of-stock items cannot be ordered.

## SHP-3 Delayed orders
If your order has passed its estimated delivery date, first check the carrier tracking link, because most delays clear within 2 business days. If the order is 2 or more business days late, contact support: we will open an investigation with the carrier and update you within 48 hours. If the parcel is 7 or more business days late, it is treated as lost and you can choose a full refund or a free replacement sent by next-day delivery.

## SHP-4 Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is processing. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed, but you may be able to redirect the parcel through the carrier's own website.

## SHP-5 Missing items
If a parcel arrives with an item missing, report it within 7 days of delivery. Orders with several items are sometimes sent in separate parcels, so check your tracking for a second shipment first.

## SHP-6 International delivery
NovaShop currently delivers only to addresses in the United Kingdom, including Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man.

## SHP-7 Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships and is also shown in your account. Carriers used are DPD, Royal Mail and Evri.
