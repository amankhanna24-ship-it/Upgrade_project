# Refunds and Exchanges

## REF-1 Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the returned item passing inspection at our warehouse. Inspection normally takes 2 business days from receipt. Your bank may take a further 3 to 5 business days to show the money in your account.

## REF-2 Restocking fees
A 10% restocking fee applies only to opened laptops, smartphones and monitors returned for a change of mind (see Returns Policy RET-2). No restocking fee applies to faulty items, items damaged on arrival, or unopened items.

## REF-3 Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different colour, size or model of equal or higher value within the return window. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference. Exchanges for opened laptops, smartphones and monitors are subject to the same 14-day limit as returns.

## REF-4 Delivery charges
Original delivery charges are refunded only when the whole order is returned because it was faulty, damaged on arrival or sent in error. Return postage is free for faulty or damaged items; for change-of-mind returns a £4.95 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

## REF-5 Refunds for warranty plans
A NovaCare or NovaCare Plus plan can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided no claim has been made. After 30 days, cancellation gives a pro-rata refund minus a £10 administration fee. If the covered product is returned for a refund, the plan attached to it is refunded in full automatically.

## REF-6 Price adjustments
If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of delivery, contact support and we will refund the difference once per order. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales or clearance items.
