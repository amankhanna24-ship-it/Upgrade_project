# NovaCare and NovaCare Plus: Extended Warranty Terms

## NC-1 What NovaCare is
NovaCare is NovaShop's extended warranty. It extends the manufacturer warranty so that the product is covered for defects in materials and workmanship for a total of 3 years from the delivery date. It costs 10% of the product price and must be bought at the same time as the product.

## NC-2 NovaCare Plus and accidental damage
NovaCare Plus includes everything in NovaCare and adds accidental damage cover: drops, cracked screens and liquid spills. It costs 18% of the product price. You can make up to 2 accidental damage claims in any 12-month period. An excess applies to each accidental damage claim: £49 for laptops and smartphones, £19 for all other products.

## NC-3 What standard NovaCare does not do
Standard NovaCare does not cover accidental damage. A laptop or phone that has been dropped, cracked or exposed to liquid is only covered if the customer has NovaCare Plus. Neither NovaCare nor NovaCare Plus extends the return window: once the return window in the Returns Policy has closed, a damaged or faulty item is handled as a repair or replacement claim, never as a change-of-mind return or refund.

## NC-4 Making a NovaCare claim
Claims are made online or through support using the order number. For laptops, monitors and phones we arrange a free courier collection; for smaller items we send a prepaid label. Repairs normally take 7 to 10 business days. If the item cannot be repaired economically, we replace it with the same or a better model. Cash refunds are not given under NovaCare, except as described in Refunds REF-5.

## NC-5 Exclusions
NovaCare and NovaCare Plus do not cover loss or theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect how the product works, deliberate damage, damage from unauthorised repairs or modifications, or consumables such as ear tips and straps.

## NC-6 Transferring a plan
A NovaCare plan stays with the product if you give it away or sell it. The new owner needs the original order number to make a claim.
